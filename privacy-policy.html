1. Introduction

Tessera is an online fashion shopping application that allows users to create an account, browse and search fashion products, add items to a cart, place orders, manage a profile, upload a profile photo, and write product reviews (with an optional single image attachment).

We take your privacy seriously. This policy is written to help users, parents or guardians, and app store reviewers understand exactly what Tessera does with personal data. By creating an account or otherwise using Tessera, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

3. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The Tessera mobile application available on supported platforms;
  • Backend services that power Tessera (Node.js application programming interfaces);
  • Personal data stored in Tessera’s database (MongoDB) and processed through our hosting provider (Railway); and
  • Communications you send to our support or contact email addresses regarding Tessera.

This policy does not cover third-party websites, stores, or services that we do not control, even if they are linked from Tessera or from an app store listing.

4. Information we collect

Tessera collects personal information that you provide directly when you register, update your profile, place an order, or submit a review. The categories of personal information we process are listed below.

4.1 Account and profile information

Full name Used to identify your account and personalize your profile.
Email address Used for registration, login, account recovery, and support communication.
Phone number Used for account contact and order-related communication where needed.
Password Collected at registration or when you change your password. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. We do not store passwords in plain text.
Gender Collected as a profile attribute to support personalization of your account.
Delivery address Collected so Tessera can record and fulfill orders you place.
Profile photo Collected only if you choose to upload one. The image is stored so it can be displayed on your Tessera profile.

4.2 Order and shopping activity

When you browse products, search the catalog, manage a cart, or place an order, Tessera processes the product selections, cart contents, and order details associated with your account so the shopping experience can function as intended.

4.3 Reviews and optional review images

If you write a product review, Tessera stores the review text and related metadata (such as the product reviewed and the time of submission). You may optionally upload one image with a review. Providing a review image is entirely optional and is not required to submit a text review.

4.4 Authentication data

Tessera uses JWT access tokens and JWT refresh tokens to authenticate sessions after you log in. These tokens are used to keep you signed in securely and to authorize requests to Tessera’s backend services.

4.5 Information we do not collect

Tessera does not collect payment card details through an in-app payment system, does not run advertising identifiers for ads, and does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current version of the application.

5. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and maintain your Tessera account;
  • Authenticate you and protect account access using JWT-based sessions;
  • Display your profile information and profile photo within Tessera;
  • Enable product browsing, search, cart management, and order placement;
  • Process and display product reviews and optional review images;
  • Respond to support requests and account deletion requests;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper operation of Tessera; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations when required.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws require a legal basis, we process personal information under one or more of the following grounds, as applicable:

  • Contract performance — to provide Tessera features you request, such as registration, login, orders, and profile management;
  • Consent — for optional uploads such as a profile photo or a review image, which you choose to provide;
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, and improve operational reliability in ways that do not override your rights; and
  • Legal obligation — when we must retain or disclose information to satisfy applicable law, regulation, or lawful request.

7. Photos and media access

Important: Tessera only uploads the image you deliberately select. The application does not scan your photo library, does not access photos you did not choose, and does not continuously monitor your device gallery.

Photo-related features in Tessera are limited to:

  • Profile photo upload — you may choose one image from your device to set as your profile photo; and
  • Optional review image — when writing a review, you may optionally attach one image that you select.

If you never choose an image, Tessera does not upload photos from your device. You may decline optional photo uploads and continue using other Tessera features that do not require them.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We may share information only in the limited circumstances described below.

8.1 Service infrastructure

Tessera’s backend is built with Node.js and stores data in MongoDB. The service is hosted on Railway. These providers process data on our behalf as infrastructure necessary to operate Tessera, subject to appropriate contractual and security controls offered by those platforms.

8.2 Legal requirements

We may disclose personal information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of Pixora, Tessera users, or the public.

8.3 Business transfers

If Pixora is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or asset transfer, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will continue to protect personal information consistent with this policy or provide notice of material changes.

9. Security measures

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption for data in transit;
  • bcrypt hashing for passwords at rest;
  • JWT access and refresh tokens for authenticated API access; and
  • Access controls and hosting practices appropriate to Tessera’s architecture on Railway.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong unique password for your Tessera account and keep your login credentials confidential.

10. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your Tessera account remains active and as needed to provide the service. If you request account deletion, we will delete or irreversibly anonymize associated personal data after verifying your identity, unless we are required to retain certain information longer to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

Operational backups and system logs, where used by our hosting environment, may retain residual copies for a limited period consistent with backup rotation and security practices, after which they are overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which can include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at tesseraapptessera@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

12. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Tessera account directly within the app at any time.

When you delete your account, your account and associated personal data will be permanently removed from our systems, except where we are required by law to retain certain information.

If you experience any issues deleting your account through the app, please contact us at tesseraapptessera@gmail.com and we will assist you.

For more information, visit our Account Deletion page.

13. Children’s privacy

Tessera is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Tessera, please contact us at tesseraapptessera@gmail.com, and we will take appropriate steps to delete such information.

14. International data transfers

Tessera may be accessed from different countries, and our hosting and infrastructure providers may process data in locations outside your country of residence. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take steps designed to ensure that appropriate protections remain in place consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

15. Features not present in Tessera

To avoid misunderstanding about Tessera’s data practices, we expressly confirm that the current version of Tessera does not include:

  • Artificial intelligence (AI) features;
  • An in-app payment system;
  • Push notifications;
  • Advertisements;
  • Analytics tracking tools; or
  • Third-party advertising SDKs.

If any of these capabilities are added in a future release, we will update this Privacy Policy before or at the time those features become available, as appropriate.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in Tessera, our practices, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within Tessera or by email. Continued use of Tessera after an updated policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy, except where applicable law requires a different standard.
