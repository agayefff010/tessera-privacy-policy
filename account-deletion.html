Delete your Tessera account

You can permanently delete your Tessera account directly within the app. This page explains what happens when you delete your account and how your personal data is handled.

Overview

You can permanently delete your Tessera account directly within the app. Account deletion is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, you will need to create a new account if you wish to use Tessera again.

If you experience any issues deleting your account, please contact tesseraapptessera@gmail.com.

In-app account deletion

Available. Users can permanently delete their Tessera account directly within the mobile application at any time.

If you encounter any issues using the in-app account deletion feature, please contact our support team for assistance.

How to delete your account

You can permanently delete your Tessera account directly within the app by following these steps:

1

Open Tessera

Sign in to your Tessera account.

2

Go to your Profile

Open your profile and navigate to the account settings.

3

Select Delete Account

Tap the Delete Account option.

4

Confirm deletion

Confirm your decision. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

5

Account removed

Your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted, except where we are legally required to retain certain information.

If you experience any issues deleting your account through the app, please contact us at tesseraapptessera@gmail.com.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account through the app, Pixora will permanently delete your Tessera account and associated personal data from active systems, including where applicable:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password hash
  • Gender
  • Delivery address
  • Profile photo
  • Account authentication records associated with your user profile
  • Cart and order records tied to your account, except where retention is legally required
  • Reviews and optional review images attributable to your account, except where retention is legally required or where reviews have been irreversibly anonymized

Once deletion is complete, you will no longer be able to sign in to Tessera with that account.

What may be retained

In limited cases, Pixora may retain certain information when required by applicable law, legal process, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our Terms & Conditions. Where retention is necessary, we limit it to what is reasonably required and do not continue to use the data for ordinary marketing or advertising purposes.

Residual copies may temporarily remain in encrypted backups or system logs maintained by our hosting infrastructure until those backups are rotated or overwritten in the ordinary course of operations.

Processing timeline

We process account deletion requests immediately after the user confirms the deletion within the app. In exceptional cases, processing may take up to 30 days due to technical, security, or legal requirements.

If we need more information to verify your identity, the timeline begins after we receive that information.
