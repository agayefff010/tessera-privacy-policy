What you can do

Everything you need to shop fashion online.

Tessera focuses on a clear shopping experience—accounts, browsing, cart, orders, and reviews—without unrelated tracking or advertising layers.

Account & profile

Register and sign in with email. Manage your profile details and optionally upload a profile photo.

Browse & search

Explore fashion products and find items quickly with search so you can discover what fits your style.

Cart & orders

Add products to your cart and place orders. Delivery address information is used to fulfill your purchases.

Reviews

Share feedback on products. You may optionally attach one image to a review—upload is always under your control.

Secure access

Authentication uses JWT access and refresh tokens. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

HTTPS by default

Tessera traffic is protected with HTTPS. Backend services run on Node.js with MongoDB data storage, hosted on Railway.

Privacy commitments

Clear about what Tessera does—and does not do.

Photo access is limited to images you choose. There are no ads, analytics SDKs, AI features, or in-app payments in the current version.

What Tessera collects

  • Full name — to personalize your account
  • Email address — for sign-in and support
  • Phone number — for order and account contact
  • Password — stored only as a bcrypt hash
  • Gender — optional profile preference
  • Delivery address — to fulfill orders
  • Profile photo — only if you upload one

Not included in Tessera

  • No AI features
  • No payment system
  • No push notifications
  • No advertisements
  • No analytics tracking
  • No third-party ad SDKs

Photo access: Tessera only uploads the image you select for a profile photo or an optional review image. The app never scans or accesses other photos on your device.

Need to delete your Tessera account?

Account deletion is not yet available inside the app. Email support to request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data after identity verification.
