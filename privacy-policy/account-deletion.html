Account management

Delete your Tessera account

Tessera users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data by email. This page explains the process for users and for Google Play / App Store review.

Overview

If you no longer want to use Tessera, you may request that Pixora permanently delete your account and the personal data associated with it. Account deletion is irreversible once completed. After deletion, you will need to create a new account if you want to use Tessera again in the future.

Support email for deletion requests: tesseraapptessera@gmail.com

In-app deletion status

Currently unavailable in the app. Users cannot delete their Tessera account from inside the mobile application at this time. Deletion must be requested by email using the process below.

This web page exists so that Tessera meets Google Play Console and Apple App Store requirements for a publicly accessible account deletion pathway that users can discover outside the application listing alone.

How to request account deletion

Follow these steps to request permanent deletion of your Tessera account:

1

Prepare your request

Use the email address linked to your Tessera account. Include enough information for us to identify your account accurately.

2

Email Tessera support

Send a message to tesseraapptessera@gmail.com with the subject line Tessera Account Deletion Request.

3

Include required details

In the email body, include your full name, the email address registered on Tessera, your phone number (if provided on your profile), and a clear statement that you want your account and personal data permanently deleted.

4

Complete identity verification

Pixora will verify that the request comes from the account holder. We may ask follow-up questions or request confirmation from the registered email address before proceeding.

5

Receive confirmation

After verification and deletion, we will confirm by email that your Tessera account and associated personal data have been permanently deleted, subject to any limited legal retention described below.

What data is deleted

After successful identity verification, Pixora will permanently delete your Tessera account and associated personal data from active systems, including where applicable:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password hash
  • Gender
  • Delivery address
  • Profile photo
  • Account authentication records associated with your user profile
  • Cart and order records tied to your account, except where retention is legally required
  • Reviews and optional review images attributable to your account, except where retention is legally required or where reviews have been irreversibly anonymized

Once deletion is complete, you will no longer be able to sign in to Tessera with that account.

What may be retained

In limited cases, Pixora may retain certain information when required by applicable law, legal process, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our Terms & Conditions. Where retention is necessary, we limit it to what is reasonably required and do not continue to use the data for ordinary marketing or advertising purposes.

Residual copies may temporarily remain in encrypted backups or system logs maintained by our hosting infrastructure until those backups are rotated or overwritten in the ordinary course of operations.

Processing timeline

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within a reasonable period after receiving a complete request from a verifiable account holder. After identity verification, we process deletion without undue delay and generally within thirty (30) days, unless a longer period is required for technical, security, or legal reasons.

If we need more information to verify your identity, the timeline begins after we receive that information.
